PAPER PRESENTATION · 2026

About this research

Design goals, methodology and responsible interpretation.

PROJECT TITLE

An LLM-Augmented CNN Framework for Real-Time Underwater Fish Species and Marine Debris Identification

This prototype follows the research presentation prepared by students at IFET College of Engineering. It brings fish, coral, disease observations and marine debris into one monitoring dashboard.

01Acquire

Upload a photograph or capture a frame from a camera.

02Preprocess

Enhance image contrast and calculate descriptive image statistics.

03Detect (optional)

Use an experimental zero-shot detector or custom marine-trained YOLO model.

04Interpret

Produce grounded, rule-based summaries and downloadable reports.

IMPLEMENTATION HONESTY

What's implemented?

Dashboard, camera capture, uploads, preprocessing, model-ready backend, annotated results, explanations, exports and local history.

What needs further research?

Task-specific datasets, Mask R-CNN segmentation, a validated fish-disease classifier, fine-grained species classification, trained model performance evaluations, and installation and testing of local Ollama for LLM text generation.

Illustrative demo labels are always marked as scripted. Zero-shot predictions are experimental and are not equivalent to a validated marine monitoring system.
